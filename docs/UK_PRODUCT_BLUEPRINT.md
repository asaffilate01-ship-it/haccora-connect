# Haccora UK product blueprint

Haccora UK is a commercial, multi-tenant food-safety management platform for UK food businesses. It combines the UK workflows prototyped in Food Safety Hub with Haccora's stronger tenancy, security, billing, evidence and native-app foundations.

## Product boundary

Haccora helps businesses create, follow and evidence a HACCP-based food-safety management system. It is designed to align with applicable official guidance, including FSA SFBB principles where appropriate. Haccora is not FSA-approved, does not replace an EHO inspection or competent management, and cannot guarantee a Food Hygiene Rating.

## Stage 1 delivered

- Multi-organization and multi-location tenancy with RLS
- Web platform and Expo iOS/Android application
- Secure offline mobile write queue and evidence capture
- HACCP plans, checks, temperatures, cleaning, allergens, training and traceability
- Scoped, time-limited inspector access and PDF evidence export
- Corrective actions and tamper-evident audit events
- Stripe subscription foundation and server-owned entitlements
- Versioned UK compliance-content model with approval and source metadata
- England, Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland jurisdiction selection
- Landlord, contractor, business and shared responsibility assignment model
- CI, security scanning, dependency review, database isolation and release evidence

## Stage 2 priorities

1. Guided SFBB-style safe-method builder and diary.
2. Ingredient-to-recipe allergen propagation and PPDS label generation.
3. UK sector packs for caterers, retailers, care homes, childminders, bakeries and mobile traders.
4. Location onboarding that creates proportionate templates from business type and processes.
5. Compliance-content administration with specialist approval and controlled publishing.
6. Complete GBP pricing, invoices, VAT handling and Stripe product catalogue.
7. EHO-qualified pilot programme and documented feedback from multiple local authorities.
