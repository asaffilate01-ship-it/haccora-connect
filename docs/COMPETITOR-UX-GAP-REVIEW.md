# Haccora UK competitor and UX gap review

Reviewed 6 August 2026 against current public product pages for Leafe, Blueroll, Lemon, Seito, Rated5, Checkit and FoodDocs.

## Patterns worth adopting

- Put today's routines and the next required action ahead of the full module catalogue.
- Keep one persistent Log action for temperatures, deliveries, cleaning, diary and incidents.
- Give every staff member immediate read-only access to current dish allergen information.
- Use short, plain-language cards, compact type and one primary action per screen.
- Make failures explicit and require corrective action rather than allowing a misleading completion.
- Keep EHO evidence export, expiry alerts and multi-site oversight available but outside high-frequency staff navigation.

## Haccora position after Phase 18

Haccora now matches the core mobile pattern for routines, temperatures, deliveries, cleaning, allergens, training, documents, alerts and inspection evidence. It exceeds many low-cost products in tenant isolation, health-record privacy, immutable attribution, controlled inspector access, PPDS versioning, staff induction and security governance.

Remaining commercial launch work is primarily operational: production deployment, store signing, billing-provider live configuration, customer support processes, penetration testing, UK data-protection documentation, independent food-safety review and pilot evidence from real premises.

## Phase 20 review — scan-to-work equipment

The market review was refreshed against Leafe plus current low-cost UK kitchen products (Seito, Blueroll, MiseOS, TempTake, Loggo and Forkto) and specialist QR asset products. This is an indicative public-site comparison, not a claim to catalogue every product or private roadmap.

- Leafe's advantage remains breadth: routines, inventory, allergens, workforce and training in one chef-focused application.
- Low-cost products are competing on very short daily logging, transparent per-site pricing and one-tap inspection packs.
- Loggo demonstrates the value of scan-to-start kitchen routines; specialist asset tools demonstrate a durable pattern of QR identity, full history, offline work and immutable audit evidence.
- Haccora should not copy generic asset-management complexity. Phase 20 applies the useful pattern specifically to food-premises equipment: protected QR lookup, compact details, inspections/service/calibration/issues, append-only history, role/location RLS and EHO evidence export.
- Phase 20 is stronger than a public QR form because a label is not an access credential: sign-in, tenant membership and optional inspector scope are still required.
