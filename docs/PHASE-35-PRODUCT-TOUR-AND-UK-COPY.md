## Claims boundary

Haccora is not FSA, Food Standards Scotland, local-authority or EHO approved or endorsed. It supports HACCP-based workflows and organised evidence; the food business remains responsible for suitable procedures, implementation, records and staff competence. An authorised officer assesses the business in context.

## Remaining production gates
