# UK compliance validation gate

Do not publish claims that Haccora is FSA-approved, EHO-approved, legally guaranteed or guaranteed to produce a five-star rating.

Before public production launch:

- [ ] A UK food-safety specialist reviews every safe method, checklist, threshold and corrective-action prompt.
- [ ] Legal counsel reviews terms, privacy, data retention, marketing claims and processor contracts.
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific content is checked for England, Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland.
- [ ] At least two caterers, two takeaways and two other food-business types complete pilot operation.
- [ ] Pilot businesses present Haccora evidence during real or arranged local-authority reviews.
- [ ] Requested changes are logged, implemented and signed off.
- [ ] Accessibility, offline operation and evidence exports are tested on physical iOS and Android devices.
- [ ] A penetration test and cross-tenant access test pass.
- [ ] Store privacy and Google data-safety declarations match actual processing.
