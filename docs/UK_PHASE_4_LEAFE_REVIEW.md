# UK Phase 4: Leafe review and guided adoption

## What was added

- A data-driven **Get started** centre for the first week of using Haccora.
- Setup progress derived from the real UK compliance profile, team memberships, active safe methods, equipment, suppliers, recipes, checks and training records.
- Trial-days visibility where the subscription provider has supplied `trial_ends_at`.
- Direct links from every incomplete step to the correct Haccora workflow.
- Smaller public-site display typography and smaller native titles, cards and supporting copy.

The setup percentage is an adoption indicator, not an official food hygiene or legal-compliance score.

## Leafe comparison

Leafe positions itself as a broad kitchen-management app, covering opening and closing routines, checklists, temperatures, inventory, waste, menus and allergens, shifts, clocking, training, HACCP support and multi-device operation. Haccora already has equivalent or deeper product areas, including controlled HACCP versions, UK safe methods, PPDS, recalls, scoped inspector access, corrective-action governance, security controls and inspection evidence packs.

Leafe's clearest advantages are not missing database modules. They are adoption and service:

- a short free trial and visible onboarding;
- a simple chef-led daily experience;
- video tutorials;
- engagement and hygiene scoring;
- named support representatives and a food-safety hotline;
- consultancy-supported HACCP, complaints and investigations.

Phase 4 addresses the first adoption gap. Haccora should not claim hotline, EHO approval, consultancy or specialist investigation services until contracted qualified people and service levels exist.

## Recommended next product work

1. Add a governed help centre with short UK-specific walkthroughs and versioned source review.
2. Add a simplified shift screen combining opening, service exceptions and closing into one flow.
3. Add multi-site comparison using evidence coverage, overdue actions and completion timeliness—never present it as an FHRS prediction.
4. Add native safe-method, PPDS and inspection-pack parity.
5. Pilot onboarding with independent restaurants, takeaways, cafés and care/school caterers across the relevant UK nations.

## Sample repository status

Haccora Connect contains every route from the legacy Haccora repository. The useful Food Safety Hub concepts—UK safe-method content, evidence photos, immutable audit history, allergens, corrective actions and inspector sharing—are represented or improved. Haccora Connect deliberately retains authenticated, expiring and scoped inspector access rather than copying anonymous public share tokens.
