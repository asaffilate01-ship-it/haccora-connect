# Haccora UK — product authority

Haccora UK is a multi-tenant food-safety operations platform for restaurant, retail and catering premises across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## Audiences and outcomes

- **Staff:** quickly record attributable checks, safe methods, temperatures, cleaning, deliveries and exceptions, including offline evidence.
- **Managers/owners:** prioritise unresolved hazards, approve corrective actions, track supplier/allergen/label changes and produce evidence packs.
- **Inspectors:** time-limited, read-only and explicitly scoped access to permissible evidence; no privilege escalation.
- **Platform operators:** reliable tenant/security/billing support without assuming visibility of individual customer's private health or evidence records.

## Product boundaries

Haccora helps document and manage food safety, not guarantee compliance, legally certify a premises or award a food hygiene rating. Failed reads, partial records and zero activity must never be presented as safe or compliant. Country/nation workflows and expert-reviewed content always override generic AI responses.

## Existing product technologies

TanStack Start, React, Tailwind, Radix/shadcn-style primitives, Supabase RLS, Expo native, Omniqora review workflows. Do not recreate components or replace the backend without a proven reason.
